# Codesk

**AI-native helpdesk for early-stage B2B SaaS.**

Codesk drafts support ticket replies for your product using retrieval over
your own codebase and docs, so one person can run customer support for an
entire SaaS company without living in Zendesk tickets all day. Every reply is
reviewed and approved by a human before it's sent — nothing goes out
automatically.

## Who it's for

- Founders doing their own customer support at 2–8 person teams
- Solo CS hires drowning in ticket volume at Series A companies

## Status

Codesk is in active development. We're currently working with a small group
of early customers before opening things up more broadly.
